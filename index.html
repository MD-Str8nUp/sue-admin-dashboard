                      Task vs deadline vs reminder

                      Task
                      An action you must complete (e.g. "Submit CPD activity log"). Lands in This week once saved via Quick Capture.
                      Deadline
                      The hard date the task is due by (e.g. "AHPRA registration expires 30 November 2026"). Shows in Upcoming deadlines.
                      Reminder
                      A lead-time nudge before the deadline (e.g. "Remind me two days before"). Attaches to the task Quick Capture creates.
